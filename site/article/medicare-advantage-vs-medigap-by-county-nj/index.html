Medicare Advantage or Medigap in NJ: how plans differ by county

Medicare Advantage choices depend on the county you live in. Medigap mostly does not. Here is how to compare what is really available at your address, with free help from NJ SHIP.

Two friends can both live in New Jersey and see very different Medicare Advantage choices. A retiree in Bergen County and one in Salem County may not have the same plans, the same networks or the same hospitals to choose from. That is because Advantage plans are sold by service area, which in New Jersey usually means by county.

Medigap works differently. This guide explains what changes from county to county, what does not, and how to compare plans at your own address. For the basics of each path, start with our Medicare Advantage vs. Medigap guide.

Medicare Open Enrollment runs October 15 to December 7, 2026. Changes take effect January 1, 2027.

Why do Medicare Advantage plans vary by county?

Each Medicare Advantage plan is approved for a specific service area. Insurers decide which counties to offer each plan in, and they build networks of doctors and hospitals there.

You can see this in NJ SHIP's chart of 2026 New Jersey Medicare Advantage plans, current as of October 28, 2025. It listed about 65 plans from nine companies. Some plans were offered in all 21 counties. Others were offered in a short list of counties, or in every county but one.

So the questions that matter are:

  • Which plans are offered in my county for 2027?
  • Which of those include my doctors and my hospital?
  • Do they cover my prescriptions at my pharmacy?

A plan a friend loves in another county may not be sold where you live. Even the same plan can have different nearby doctors in different counties.

Near a state line?

Many New Jersey residents get care in New York City or Philadelphia. If that is you, check whether each plan's network includes those doctors and hospitals. With Original Medicare and Medigap, you can see any doctor or hospital in the country that accepts Medicare.

Does Medigap cost more in some counties?

Mostly no, based on the state's own rate chart. NJ SHIP's September 2026 chart of Medigap premiums at age 65 lists 18 insurers. Almost all show a single statewide price for each plan letter. One company on that chart charged a different rate in certain Newark ZIP codes.

What does vary is the company and the plan letter. Prices for the same plan letter can be quite different from one insurer to the next. NJ SHIP also notes that rates can change during the year with approval from the NJ Department of Banking and Insurance, and that some companies offer household discounts. Always confirm the price with the company.

The bigger Medigap issue in New Jersey is timing. Your best chance to buy is the six-month Medigap Open Enrollment Period that starts when you are 65 and enrolled in Part B. After that, companies can ask health questions. Our Advantage vs. Medigap guide explains the rules, including New Jersey's coverage for people under 65 on Medicare due to disability.

How do I compare plans at my address?

Step 1: Use Medicare Plan Finder

  1. Go to Medicare Plan Finder on Medicare.gov. Enter your ZIP code.
  2. Enter your drugs, with the exact doses, and pick one or two pharmacies you use.
  3. Compare total yearly cost, not only the premium. Look at the drug costs, the deductible and the plan's yearly out-of-pocket limit.
  4. Check the star ratings and whether the plan is an HMO or a PPO.

Prefer the phone? Call 1-800-MEDICARE, 1-800-633-4227 (TTY 1-877-486-2048), any time, day or night, except some federal holidays.

Step 2: Call your doctors

Plan Finder cannot promise a doctor will stay in a network. Call each doctor's billing office and ask if they will be in network for the exact plan name for 2027.

Step 3: Get a free second opinion from NJ SHIP

The State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP) gives free, unbiased Medicare counseling. Counselors do not sell anything and do not recommend specific products. They help you understand your options and compare plans. NJ SHIP also publishes the state's Medicare Advantage, Part D and Medigap rate charts.

Compare at your own ZIP code. A plan your friend has may not be sold where you live.

What if I move to another county?

If you move out of your Medicare Advantage plan's service area, you get a Special Enrollment Period. Medicare says your chance to switch starts when you move and lasts two full months after. If you tell your plan before you move, it can start the month before.

During that time you can join a new Advantage plan or drug plan, or go back to Original Medicare. If you do nothing, you will be put in Original Medicare when your old plan drops you. Moving out of a plan's area can also give you a guaranteed right to buy certain Medigap policies.

The same Special Enrollment Period applies if you move within your plan's area but have new plan options at your new address. This matters if a parent moves in with you, into senior housing in another county, or to the shore. See our housing options guide for more on moving.

Help paying for Medicare

If money is tight, ask SHIP about programs that lower Medicare and drug costs. New Jersey's PAAD and Senior Gold programs help with prescriptions, and some Advantage plans have lower premiums for people with PAAD, Medicaid or Extra Help. See our guide to prescription and bill help. New to Medicare? Start with Medicare at 65.

Common questions

Are Medicare Advantage plans different in each New Jersey county?

Yes. Each plan is approved for a service area, and many New Jersey plans are offered only in certain counties. Enter your ZIP code in Medicare Plan Finder to see what is available at your address.

Do Medigap premiums vary by county in New Jersey?

Mostly no. On NJ SHIP's September 2026 chart for age 65, nearly every insurer listed one statewide price per plan. Prices do vary a lot between companies, so compare.

How do I compare Medicare plans in my county?

Use Medicare Plan Finder at medicare.gov/plan-compare with your ZIP code, drugs and pharmacies, or call 1-800-633-4227. For free unbiased help, call NJ SHIP at 1-800-792-8820.

What happens to my Medicare Advantage plan if I move to another county?

If you move out of the plan's service area, you can switch plans or return to Original Medicare. Medicare says the window starts when you move and lasts two full months after.

Is NJ SHIP counseling really free?

Yes. NJ SHIP counseling is free and unbiased. Counselors do not sell insurance or recommend specific products. Help is available in all 21 counties.
